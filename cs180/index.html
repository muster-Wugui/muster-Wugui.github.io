CS 180: Computer Vision & Computational Photography

This page is for my CS 180 work. Every project I finish gets a write-up here with the images and a short explanation of what I did. Links go up as I go.

The class

CS 180 is about doing things with images — filtering them, warping them, stitching them together, and generating new ones. It mixes classic algorithms with deep learning, and most of the work is projects done in Python.
